Empowering Girls Online 💜

Free digital rights, data protection & online safety training for girls and young women.

Welcome to your safe digital space

Dada Safe is a free platform supporting girls and young women with digital rights education, data protection awareness, fact-checking skills, and psychosocial support to navigate the internet safely. If you have ever felt unsafe, unheard, or unsure how to respond to online abuse, you belong here.
